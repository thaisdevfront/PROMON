PROMON S.A.

Promon S.A. is the holding company that operates in engineering and technology, defining strategic guidelines, corporate governance and sustainability for the companies in its portfolio. Through this structure, it maintains alignment and integration among its different businesses.

ESG AT PROMON IN 2026

Discover the environmental, social and governance practices and initiatives of Promon S.A.'s companies in 2026.

Promon S.A.

  • Conducting a Diversity Census with professionals from Promon S.A., Promon Engenharia and Forzy.
  • Promoting dialogue through meetings of the diversity groups Affinity LGBTQIAPN+, Generational Connections, Racial and Gender Equity, and Parenthood, strengthening spaces for listening, sharing experiences and collectively developing initiatives.
  • Advancing the organization's ESG maturity level, with an increase in its Ethos Indicators score from 4.3 in 2025 to 5.1 in 2026.

Promon Engenharia

  • Progress in implementing ESG performance indicator management tools and developing the greenhouse gas emissions inventory.
  • Incorporation of criteria such as energy efficiency and resource use into its projects.
  • Launch of the Parenthood Guide to raise leaders' awareness of the topic.

Forzy

  • Operations focused on asset digitalization and improved operational management, contributing to the optimization of clients' resource use, with positive impacts on areas such as energy consumption, raw material use and monitoring of environmental indicators, including emissions measurement and carbon footprint.
  • Supporting clients in production planning, optimizing installed capacity and reducing rework, with significant impacts on resource use.

Logicalis Latin America

  • 100% of Latin American operations with greenhouse gas emissions management.
  • Expansion of diversity, equity and inclusion initiatives, with affinity groups, training programs and structured engagement activities, reinforcing the commitment to a more inclusive workplace.
  • Launch of the Volunteer Platform, which brings together initiatives across the region and already has more than 500 participants.
